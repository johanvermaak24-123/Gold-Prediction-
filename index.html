Protection logic

Anti-flip gate: an opposite bias must survive two consecutive qualified scans before the accepted direction flips.

Event deduplication: repeated copies of one news event cannot fake consensus.

Learning gate: feature weights stay neutral until at least 12 real resolved samples exist; source weights wait for 20.

Ambiguous bars: if a 5-minute candle could have hit both TP and invalidation, that forecast is excluded from learning.
