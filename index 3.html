Protection logic

Duplicate control: repeated copies of one story are clustered and discounted.

DEEP NEWS EVIDENCE

Global + official stories feeding the prediction

The scanner pulls the latest 24 hours of gold, USD, Fed, yields, inflation, jobs, geopolitics, ETF/central-bank and risk-market coverage from global and regional feeds, then deduplicates repeated stories before scoring them.

Important: this is probabilistic research software, not a guaranteed forecast. It will not claim certainty.
